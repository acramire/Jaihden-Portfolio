# Jaihden Torres Website Build Plan

Last updated: 2026-06-20

## 1. Direction Update From Client Conversation

This plan updates the earlier creator-hub direction based on the Apple Messages conversation with Jaihden.

Key decisions from the thread:

- Tone should be **balanced but professional**.
- The site should take inspiration from online coaching funnels, specifically the flow, questionnaire, optional call, what-you-get section, pictures, and testimonials.
- Public copy should say **bio optimization**, not peptides.
- Do not mention peptides directly in public-facing website copy.
- UofPump should be treated as a **co-equal brand**, not only a light mention.
- Testimonials should be included as quote-style proof.
- The YouTube video **Beer Tastes Better With Abs** should be used as source material for selective conversion copy because Jaihden said it explains his story and what clients get from coaching.
- The website should use pieces of his story to help conversions and show what he is like as a coach, but it should not become a full autobiography.
- Calendly exists as a booking path for a 30-minute meeting.

## 2. Strategic Reframe

The site should be rebuilt as a **coaching conversion funnel with creator and brand support**, not only as a general creator hub.

Primary goal:

- Convert visitors into qualified coaching leads.

Secondary goals:

- Establish trust in Jaihden as a coach and personality.
- Present UofPump as a co-equal part of Jaihden's ecosystem.
- Send visitors to relevant social/content channels.
- Support brand/collaboration inquiries without distracting from coaching.

## 3. Tone And Copy Rules

Use a tone that is:

- Confident
- Direct
- Youthful
- Fitness-first
- Social-lifestyle aware
- Professional enough for serious buyers, parents, brands, and referral traffic

Avoid:

- Direct profanity in main website copy
- Overly aggressive frat-gatekeeping language
- Medical claims that are not reviewed
- Direct peptide claims or promotion
- Copy that feels like an unfiltered Instagram caption pasted into a business site

Acceptable language:

- `Fratlete`
- `Build muscle. Burn fat. Balance your social life.`
- `College lifestyle coaching`
- `Bio optimization`
- `Look better, feel better, perform better.`
- `For guys who want results without disappearing from their social life.`

Use raw Instagram language only as inspiration. Rewrite it into cleaner conversion copy.

## 4. Recommended Site Structure

### 4.1 Hero

Purpose:

- Immediately position Jaihden as the coach for social, college-lifestyle fitness.

Content:

- Jaihden's name
- Short positioning line
- Primary CTA: `Apply for Coaching`
- Secondary CTA: `Watch The Story` or `Shop UofPump`
- Visual: strong Jaihden photo or reel still

Recommended hero copy direction:

```text
Fitness coaching for guys who want to get leaner, stronger, and more confident without disappearing from their social life.
```

### 4.2 Qualification / Questionnaire Flow

Purpose:

- Turn casual visitors into qualified leads.

Required behavior:

- CTA opens or links to a coaching application/questionnaire.
- Questionnaire should collect enough information to decide whether the visitor is a fit.
- After questionnaire submission, route qualified leads to an optional Calendly call.

Recommended fields:

- Name
- Email
- Phone or Instagram handle
- Age
- Current training experience
- Primary goal
- Current biggest obstacle
- Timeline or event deadline
- Social/lifestyle constraints
- Interest in bio optimization
- Budget/readiness indicator
- Preferred contact method

### 4.3 What You Get

Purpose:

- Explain the actual coaching deliverables clearly.

Possible items:

- Custom training plan
- Nutrition targets and guidance
- Custom meal plan and grocery guidance
- Weekly check-ins
- Weekly one-on-one video coaching calls
- Lifestyle strategy for nights out and travel
- Social protocols for alcohol, parties, travel, and high-calorie weekends
- Progress tracking
- Accountability
- Bio optimization guidance
- Direct access/support boundaries, potentially via personal number or messaging access
- Phased coaching plan, with onboarding/foundation, momentum, and longer-term standard phases

This section should be specific enough that visitors understand the offer without needing to DM first.

### 4.4 Story / Coach Fit Section

Purpose:

- Use selected story points from **Beer Tastes Better With Abs** to show why Jaihden is credible and relatable.

Guidance:

- Do not transcribe the whole life story into the page.
- Extract 3-5 conversion-relevant story beats after the video is transcribed.
- Tie each beat to what it means for clients.

Example framing:

- He understands starting from zero.
- He has made the mistakes clients are trying to avoid.
- He built a lifestyle around both fitness and social life.
- His coaching is based on lived experience, not just theory.
- He is not positioning clients for bodybuilding; he is positioning them to look, feel, and perform better in real life.
- He sees fitness as connected to confidence, money, relationships, and social life.

### 4.5 Testimonials / Proof

Purpose:

- Add trust and reduce skepticism.

Content needed from Jaihden:

- Quote-style testimonials
- Client first names or initials
- Optional before/after photos
- Optional screenshots, with permission

Requirement:

- Do not fabricate testimonials.
- Use exact quotes only when provided.

### 4.6 Bio Optimization

Purpose:

- Include the health optimization angle without public peptide language.

Copy rule:

- Use `bio optimization`.
- Do not use `peptides` in public copy unless Jaihden later explicitly approves and the copy has compliance review.

Recommended placement:

- Mention as an optional coaching layer or partner/resource link.
- Keep it secondary to training, nutrition, and lifestyle coaching.

### 4.7 UofPump / Pump University

Purpose:

- Present UofPump as the co-equal brand side of Jaihden's ecosystem and drive shop traffic.

Placement:

- Strong dedicated section and persistent navigation presence.
- UofPump should appear as one of the two major pillars: coaching and brand.

Content:

- Pump University / UofPump brand intro
- Trademark Collection
- `Look Great. Feel Better.`
- `Commit Now`
- Product imagery
- CTA to shop UofPump

### 4.8 Content Hub

Purpose:

- Show Jaihden's personality and social proof.

Content:

- Featured YouTube video: **Beer Tastes Better With Abs**
- Selected reels/posts
- UofPump launch/content clips
- Optional embedded YouTube content

Implementation note:

- Prefer manually managed featured content cards over relying only on Instagram embeds, because Instagram public pages can show login overlays.

### 4.9 Contact / Collabs

Purpose:

- Preserve non-coaching opportunities without distracting from the main funnel.

Paths:

- Coaching: application/questionnaire
- Brand/collab: contact form
- Shopping: UofPump link
- Social: Instagram/YouTube links

## 5. Functional Requirements

- The homepage shall prioritize the coaching application CTA above social/shop CTAs.
- The site shall include a questionnaire/application flow for coaching leads.
- The site shall support an optional Calendly call after qualification.
- The site shall include a `What You Get` section for coaching deliverables.
- The site shall include a quote-style testimonials section.
- The site shall include a story-based coaching section derived from the transcribed YouTube video.
- The site shall include UofPump as a co-equal brand section.
- The site shall link externally to UofPump rather than rebuilding ecommerce.
- The site shall use `bio optimization` language and avoid public `peptides` language.
- The site shall include social links to Instagram and YouTube.
- The site shall work cleanly on mobile first.
- The site shall include analytics for major CTAs.
- The site shall include SEO and Open Graph metadata.
- The site shall avoid publishing unreviewed pricing, refund promises, medical-adjacent claims, or 24-hour access promises until Jaihden confirms those details.

- Calendly handoff
- UofPump section
- Testimonials section
- Featured content section
- Contact/collab form
