Who This Is For. Who It Isn't.

The OBHealth reference confirms the page needs a direct qualification section. Jaihden's raw language should be translated into a premium, ad-safe version without losing the work-hard, play-hard edge.

This is for you if

  • You are 25+ and a high-functioning professional, business owner, breadwinner, or socialite.
  • You want to feel like you are in your prime again while still socializing, networking, and enjoying weekends.
  • You have tried cookie-cutter diets or coaches who handed you a template instead of true 1-on-1 guidance.
  • You want data-driven nutrition and bio-optimization tools instead of rigid food rules.
  • You have the budget and seriousness for premium coaching.

It isn't for you if

  • You want a cheap app subscription or quick 12-week shred and out.
  • You expect a coach to cook your meals, spoon-feed the process, or watch every rep.
  • You spend heavily on nightlife but do not want to invest in your health or physique.
  • You are unwilling to put in effort, take ownership, or make smarter weekend choices.
